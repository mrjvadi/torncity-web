Credit line: "City pack 3" by Pasha (https://sketchfab.com/Pasha.), Sketchfab Standard licence.

Modifications: individual buildings separated from the city mesh, SketchUp inches converted
to metres, back-faces removed, geometry cut at ground level and each building's facades
baked into a single small atlas (512 px, or 256 px for small buildings) embedded in a GLB.
